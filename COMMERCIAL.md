## Free: noncommercial use, with attribution

You may use, copy, change and share Gemina, free of charge, for any
noncommercial purpose. The licence names these:

* Personal use: study, research, experiments, testing, hobby projects, private

* Use by charities, schools and universities, public research bodies, public
  safety and health bodies, environmental bodies and government institutions.

When you share Gemina, or a changed copy of it, you must:

1. Include the licence text, or a link to it
   (https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).
2. Keep every line that starts with `Required Notice:` (the one in
   [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE)). That line credits
   **Riera Technologies Ltd**. Do not remove or hide it.

## Paid: commercial use needs a licence

If you want to use Gemina to make money, or inside a company for its business,
you need a paid commercial licence from Riera Technologies Ltd. Examples:

* Running Gemina to keep a business's calls or systems online.
* Offering Gemina, or a gateway built from it, as a paid or hosted service.
* Putting Gemina inside a product you sell.
* Using it at work, in a company that is not one of the bodies listed above.

If you are not sure whether your use is commercial, ask first. Do not guess.

## How to ask

Open an issue on the repository and use the title **Commercial licence
request**. Say who you are, what you want to do with Gemina and roughly how many
people or devices are involved. Do not put secrets or private data in the issue.
We reply on the issue.
